# Lantern of Reason: An Enlightenment Quest

A browser RPG for 7th-grade social studies. Students explore a four-region map, battle Fog Goons with one-question challenges, and face Thomas Hobbes, John Locke, Baron de Montesquieu and Jean-Jacques Rousseau in boss trials before taking on the Absolute King.

**Key terms covered:** social contract, state of nature, natural rights, consent of the governed, absolute and limited power, separation of powers, checks and balances, general will, popular sovereignty, democracy, republic, suffrage.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install, no accounts.

## Modes

- **Quest:** the full adventure, with optional Minerva training, upgrades and buffs.
- **Royal Rumble:** a timed round in a small library against computer rivals. You roam, duel each rival once per round, and the top 3 make the podium.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

Settings → Pages → Branch: `main`, folder: `/ (root)` → Save. After about a minute the game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.
