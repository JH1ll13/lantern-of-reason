# Lantern of Reason: An Enlightenment Quest

A browser RPG for 7th-grade social studies. Students explore a four-region map, battle Fog Goons with one-question challenges, and face Thomas Hobbes, John Locke, Baron de Montesquieu and Jean-Jacques Rousseau in boss trials before taking on the Absolute King.

**Key terms covered:** social contract, state of nature, natural rights, consent of the governed, absolute and limited power, separation of powers, checks and balances, general will, popular sovereignty, democracy, republic, suffrage.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install, no accounts.

## What makes it a battle

- **Quick Strike:** answer before the timer drains for +1 damage.
- **Combos and criticals:** every 5 correct answers in a row lands a critical hit.
- **Reason Meter and special moves:** correct answers charge the meter; when it's full, unleash a special. Each philosopher's relic unlocks a new one (Leviathan Crush, Rights Rally, Triple-Branch Strike, General Will Wave).
- **Boss phases:** the Absolute King gets furious at half health and switches to real-world application questions.

## Questions

- Goon battles ask one short question ("Social contract?").
- Trials and the final boss mix in "Apply it" scenarios that make students tell the thinkers apart (for example, "A king imprisons critics without trial. Which principle does this violate?").
- Answer positions are reshuffled so the correct choice never sits in the same spot twice in a row, and many terms have more than one scenario.

## Progression

Levels and perks, rank titles (Curious Novice to Enlightened Mind), region bounties (banish 5 goons for permanent rewards), philosopher relics, and rematches to earn a Gold medal and permanent upgrade.

## Modes

- **Quest:** the full adventure, with optional Minerva training, upgrades and buffs.
- **Royal Rumble:** a timed round in a small library against computer rivals. You roam, duel each rival once per round, and the top 3 make the podium. If every duel is finished before time runs out, the round ends early and the champion is crowned.
